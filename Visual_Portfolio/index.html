Research Focus

Leveraging evolutionary signals across ~2,000 eukaryotic genomes to predict gene function and disease associations.

Phylogenetic Profiling

utilizing Normalized Phylogenetic Profiling (NPP) to cluster genes that co-evolved, indicating functional relationships and participation in shared biological pathways.

Multi-Omics Integration

Bridging the gap between genotype and phenotype by integrating evolutionary data with Transcriptomics, Proteomics, and Phenomics (HPO) datasets.

Target Diseases

Specifically focused on uncovering novel candidates for Inherited Retinal Diseases (IRD) and Ciliopathies, addressing the "missing heritability" challenge.
