Focus: Evolutionary gene clustering, multi-omics integration, and phylogenetic profiling (IRD & Cilia).

Overview

Developing computational frameworks to address missing heritability in genetic disorders.

By integrating Normalized Phylogenetic Profiling (NPP) with multi-omics datasets, I aim to uncover conserved gene modules for Inherited Retinal Diseases (IRD) and Ciliopathies.

Note: All datasets in this repository are synthetic, transformed, or strictly demonstrative for confidentiality.
