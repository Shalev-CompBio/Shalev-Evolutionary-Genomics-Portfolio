I am a computational biologist working at the intersection of evolution, data science, and human disease.
I focus on uncovering genetic patterns that traditional approaches often miss, especially in Inherited Retinal Diseases (IRDs) and ciliopathies.
By combining evolutionary signals with multi-omics data - from gene expression to phenotypes - I identify groups of genes that work together and highlight new candidates that may contribute to disease.
My goal is to turn complex genomic data into clear biological insights that help explain why certain diseases remain unsolved.

The Core Method

Normalized Phylogenetic Profiling (NPP)

We hypothesize that genes functioning together share evolutionary histories ("Functional interdependence implies co-evolution"). By constructing phylogenetic profiles across ~2,000 eukaryotic species, we identify co-evolving modules to prioritize candidate genes for unsolved IRD cases.

Guilt by Association

Notice how Gene A and Gene B have identical presence/absence patterns across species? This suggests they are functionally linked and part of the same biological pathway.

Input: 2,000+ Eukaryotic Genomes

Process: BLASTp + Normalization

Output: Novel IRD Candidates
